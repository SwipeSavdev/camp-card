## Description

*Up to 4000 characters.*

```
Camp Card brings the beloved BSA Camp Card fundraising program into the digital age. Support your local Scout troop while enjoying exclusive discounts at participating merchants in your community.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Purchase a Camp Card subscription to support a Scout
2. Browse exclusive offers from local merchants
3. Show your digital card or QR code to redeem discounts
4. Track your savings and see the impact you're making

SUPPORT SCOUTS, SAVE MONEY

Every Camp Card purchase directly supports Scout fundraising. When you buy through a Scout's referral link, a portion goes directly to their troop, funding camping trips, merit badge programs, and community service projects.

KEY FEATURES

• Digital Discount Card - Access your Camp Card anytime on your phone
• Exclusive Merchant Offers - Save at restaurants, retail stores, services, and more
• Easy QR Code Redemption - Simply show your code to redeem offers
• Scout Attribution - See which Scout you're supporting with each purchase
• Gift Cards to Friends & Family - Share the savings and spread the support
• Track Your Savings - See how much you've saved throughout the year
• Find Nearby Merchants - Discover participating businesses in your area

MULTIPLE CARDS, MORE IMPACT

Purchase 1 to 10 Camp Cards at once. Keep one for yourself and gift the rest to friends and family. Each card supports Scouting and provides a full year of savings.

FOR SCOUTS & TROOP LEADERS

Scouts can share their unique referral link or QR code. When someone purchases a Camp Card through their link, the Scout and their troop receive credit for the fundraising.

Troop Leaders can track their troop's fundraising progress, manage Scout rosters, and monitor overall campaign performance.

ABOUT CAMP CARD

The Camp Card program has been a cornerstone of Scout fundraising for decades. Now, with the Camp Card app, the tradition continues in a modern, convenient digital format that makes it easier than ever to support Scouting while saving money in your community.

PRIVACY & SECURITY

• Secure payments processed through Apple In-App Purchase
• Your personal information is protected
• COPPA compliant for users under 13
• No credit card information stored on our servers

Download Camp Card today and start saving while supporting the next generation of Scout leaders!
```
